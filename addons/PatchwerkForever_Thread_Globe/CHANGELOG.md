# Patchwerk Forever: Globe (Thread)

## 1.2.0

- **A quest log beside the globe**: **Show Quest Log** (off as it comes), the **Quest Log** button, `/globe quests` and a key binding put a list of your quests at the right of the window, as the world map's quest log sits beside its map. The globe's picture is narrower while it shows; a window too narrow for both is widened, and goes back to its size when the list is hidden. It is also in the Show menu.
  - Your quests are listed under their headings, as the game's quest log has them, each with its level, its objectives, and Complete once it is done. Click a heading to fold it. The list shows how many quests you have, out of how many you can hold.
  - Click a quest and the globe turns to where it is done, and its mark there is picked out, larger and ringed. Clicking does not change your route.
  - Right-click a quest for **Show On Globe**, **Route To Quest** (or **Stop Routing To Quest**), **Quest Details**, **Track Quest** (or **Untrack Quest**), **Share Quest** while you are in a group, and **Add To My Route**. The quest your route follows has a gold dot.
  - With Dungeon Journal (Stitch), the stops of its list are under your quests: the dungeon quests to pick up and turn in, in the order it would take them. Click one and the globe turns to it; right-click for **Follow This List** (or **Back To Your Main Route**) and **Add To My Route**.
  - Keys: **Show Or Hide The Quest Log**, and **Next Quest In The Quest Log**, which picks each quest of the list in turn and turns the globe to it. Console Port lists both among its own.
- **Tracker (Thread)'s list stays where you keep it**: it is no longer moved beside the globe while the globe is open, and the globe's window is drawn over it. **Tracker Beside The Globe** is gone, from the options and from the Route button's list, now that the quest log is part of the globe.

## 1.1.0

- **Zones colored by level**: **Color Zones By Level** (off as it comes) lays a thin wash of color over each zone, in the colors the levels by a zone's name are written in: gray for far below you, green, yellow, orange, and red for far above. A small key in the lower left corner says which is which. It follows the zone's shape, changes when you gain a level, fades out toward the ground, and is not drawn on a city's or battleground's map. A zone the game gives no levels for has no wash, nor have the few zones the game's map has no pictures of. In the Show menu, by `/globe levels`, and by a key binding.
- **Guild members and friends**: **Show Guild Members** and **Show Friends** (both off as they come, and both in the Show menu) put one mark by a zone's name for the guild members and friends who are in it, with how many. Point at it for their names in class colors and their levels; click it to fit the zone. Battle.net friends are counted while they play this game. Yourself and anyone offline are not counted, and someone who is both is counted once.
- **A new ring**: the globe now sits in a brass ring with the four points of the compass on it. The stand is gone.
- **Your map pin and tracked quest** are read from Patchwerk Forever 2.9.0 when it has them, so the globe shows yours, not the marker Routes (Thread) moves along its trail (Mark The Way Ahead).
- **Smoother zone borders**: the borders between zones, and the outline drawn round the zone you point at, are smooth lines now. They were stepped, which showed when zoomed in close.
- **Fog of war**: land you have not discovered now lies under a thin fog, not blank paper: the painted map shows through it. The fog thins out to mist at its edges and over the shore, and clears as you explore. **Fog Opacity** sets how much it hides (30% as it comes). The setting is now called **Fog Of War** (it was Leave Unexplored Land Blank).
- **The ground is part of the fog of war**: zoomed in to where the minimap's pictures of the ground come in, they come in only over land you have discovered. The rest stays the painted map under its fog.
- **Zones you have never entered are under fog too.** Only the zones you had been in were covered, and the rest of the world was shown in full.
- **The map key opens the globe where you are**: with **The Map Key Opens The Globe** on, the key opens the globe turned and zoomed to your place. Opened any other way, the globe is as you left it.
- **Thinner outlines on names**: the names had an outline and a shadow at once, which made small letters heavy and hard to read. They have the outline alone now. **Name Outline** can change it to a shadow alone.
- **Numbers sit in the middle of their marks**: the numbers of your route's stops and of quests were drawn with the same doubled edge, which made them look off-center.
- **Smoother turning and zooming**: the globe does about half the work it did each time it is drawn while you turn or zoom it.

## 1.0.0

The first release. Needs Patchwerk Forever 2.8.0 or later.

- **The world on a globe**: the world of WoW Forever on a parchment globe you can spin and zoom, from the whole world on its stand down to the ground. `/globe` opens it.
- **Painted by this addon's own tools** from the game's terrain: each zone in a tint of its own, hills, an inked coastline, zone borders, and ink drawings on the open sea. Closer in, each continent in finer detail, then the minimap's pictures of the ground.
- **Everything the world map shows**: where you are, your quests, the map pin, flight points, boats, zeppelins, the tram and the lifts, towns and landmarks, your group, your corpse, rares and treasures, and the pins other addons put on the world map through HereBeDragons.
- **Land you have not discovered** is left as blank paper, as on the world map.
- **Cities and battlegrounds** open as flat maps in the same window, with the same marks.
- **In place of the world map**, if you want: **The Map Key Opens The Globe** (off as it comes) gives it the map key, and each map has a button to the other.
- **With Routes (Thread)**, your route is drawn on the globe, with its boats, zeppelins and recorded herb and ore nodes. **With Tracker (Thread)**, that list can sit beside the globe.
- **Light and movement**: candlelight, the Maelstrom turning, cloud shadows, aged paper. Each has a setting.
- **Keys and gamepad** for everything the mouse does, and Console Port lists the globe's bindings among its own.

## 0.11.0

- **Now named Patchwerk Forever: Globe (Thread)**: the name comes first and what kind of addon it is after it, in brackets (it was Thread (Globe)). The options page is named the same way.
- **Aged paper**: the globe looks its age. Water stains with their tide lines, clusters of foxing, grime round the poles and paler patches where the paper is rubbed, most along the equator where a hand turns it. Closer to, hairline cracks in the varnish, fine specks, and the seams between the twelve strips its paper is cut in. It lies over the map, the ink drawings and the blank paper alike, and fades away as you zoom in to the ground. **Aged Paper** turns it off (on the options page and in the Show menu). Parchment style only.
- **The ink drawings** on the sea (the compass rose, the serpents, the ships, the Maelstrom) have a pale wash under their bodies, so the sea no longer shows through them as through glass, and their ink is a little faded.
- The second ship faces the other way, as the second serpent does.

## 0.10.0

Everything the world map does. To be played with before it goes out as 1.0.

- **Land you have not discovered** is blank paper, as on the world map: the coast is there, the rest is not. It fills in as you discover it, while the window is open too. A zone you have seen nothing of is one blank sheet while you are zoomed out. **Leave Unexplored Land Blank** turns it off. Parchment style only.
- **Rares, treasures and events**: what the game marks on the minimap while they are near you, with the game's own icon; a dead one is grey (**Show Rares And Treasures**).
- **The Show menu** (a **Show** button, and a Show Menu key): each kind of mark, the names, the coordinates, the blank paper, and the light and movement, turned off and on where you look at them. They are the settings of the options page.
- **Other addons' pins**: the pins other addons put on the world map through the pin library they share (HereBeDragons) are on the globe too, once you zoom in: quest helpers, guides, rare finders. Each keeps its own picture, tooltip and click (**Show Other Addons' Pins**). An addon that draws on the world map in a way of its own is not seen.
- **City maps**: a city's own map, flat in the globe's window, with the same marks on it. It opens when you open the globe or press Where I Am inside a city (**City Maps** turns that off), from Go To, or by a click on a city that already fills the picture. A right-click, Step Out, zooming out or the **Globe** button goes back to the globe, turned to the city. With keys, Next Mark moves the cross from mark to mark.
- **Battleground maps**, the same way: you and your group, the battleground's places (graveyards, towers and the like) and its flags.
- **A guard's directions**: the flag the game puts on the map when a city guard tells you the way, on the globe and on the city's map (with **Show Places**).
- **The map key** (**The Map Key Opens The Globe**, off as it comes): the key that opens the world map opens the globe, and a map link in chat or a "show on map" turns the globe to the place. The key can only be handed over outside combat.
- **From one to the other**: a **World Map** button on the globe (and in Go To) opens the world map; a **Globe** button on the world map opens the globe at the map you were looking at.
- **Light and movement**, each with a setting:
  - **Candlelight**: the corners of the picture darken as by a candle, flickering a little, and more at night by the game clock. A soft shine lies on the ball while the whole globe is in view.
  - **Moving Sea And Sky**: the Maelstrom turns in its place, and cloud shadows drift over the globe while it is zoomed out.
  - **Animate Opening**: the window fades in and the globe turns into place.
  - **Pulse Where You Are**: a ring spreads from your place every few seconds.
  - **Map Sounds**: the world map's sounds when the globe opens and closes.
- Places that belong to an event going on now are shown with the others.
- The second sea serpent is a drawing of its own and faces the other way, so the two are not the same.
- The options page is in groups: The Map, Marks, Light And Movement, Controls, Other.
- The buttons along the top of the window are narrower, to fit the smallest window.

## 0.5.1

- **The Deeprun Tram**: for the Alliance, a tram mark at Stormwind and at Ironforge ("Tram to Ironforge", and the wait), with a dashed line between them where it runs under the ground. It comes from Routes (Thread) 1.3.0, which now knows the tram, and is shown with the boats and zeppelins (**Show Boats And Zeppelins**).
- **Lifts**: a lift mark where each lift Routes (Thread) knows stands: the Great Lift, the lift of Freewind Post and those of Thunder Bluff. They show as you zoom in, with the flight points, and go with **Show Boats And Zeppelins**.

## 0.5.0

Places, people and words.

- **Places**: the towns and landmarks of the zone at the middle of the picture, and its dungeon and raid entrances, with the game's own icons and names, as you zoom in (**Show Places**).
- **While you are dead**: your corpse, and the spirit healer the game would send you to.
- **Your group**: the members of your party or raid, each in the colour of their class, grey when dead (**Show Your Group**). A member inside a dungeon has no place on the globe and is not shown.
- **A zone's levels** beside its name at the top of the picture, coloured as the game colours them for your level.
- **The smaller place** under the pointer (a town, a camp, a pass) is named under the zone's name, once you have zoomed in to a zone.
- **Coordinates**: yours, and those of the place under the pointer, at the lower edge of the picture, on the zone's own map (**Show Coordinates**). With keys or a gamepad, those of the middle of the picture.

## 0.4.0

Quests.

- **Your quests**: a numbered mark where each quest's next objective is, a question mark where it is handed in once it is done. Point at one for its objectives.
- **Click a quest** and Routes (Thread) goes there first, as a click on it in Tracker (Thread) does; click it again to go back to your main route. The quest the route follows is larger, with a soft ring round where it is done.
- **Quests on offer** in the zone at the middle of the picture: an exclamation mark where the game says one can be picked up.
- **Your guide's steps**: where the guide you run sends you now and its next steps, numbered. A side list (the quests of Dungeon Journal (Stitch)) shows each of its quests; a click follows that list.
- **The Route button** (and a Route Menu key): what the route follows, Add Map Pin To My Route, Edit My Route, and Tracker Beside The Globe.
- **Tracker (Thread)'s list beside the globe** while it is open; it goes back where you keep it when the globe closes. A quest clicked in the list turns the globe to it. (**Tracker Beside The Globe**)
- **Show Quests** turns the quest marks off and on.

## 0.3.0

Travel and your route.

- **Marks and lines on the globe**: an icon at a place, with a tooltip and what a click does. Marks on the far side are hidden, and where they crowd as you zoom out the more important one is kept.
- **Flight points** of your side, as you zoom in; those you have not discovered are grey (**Show Flight Points**).
- **Boats and zeppelins** you can take: where they put in, what they go to and how long the wait is, and the way they go across the sea (**Show Boats And Zeppelins**; needs Routes (Thread)).
- **Your route**: the trail Routes (Thread) leads you along, in its own colours; a flight is a dashed arc over the globe, a boat a dashed line across the sea. My Route's stops are numbered; click one for Go Here First, Move Earlier, Move Later and Remove From My Route (**Show Your Route**).
- **Herb and ore nodes** Routes (Thread) has recorded, zoomed right in (**Show Herb And Ore Nodes**).
- **The map pin**: Ctrl-click sets it, as on the game's map. A click on the pin tracks it or stops tracking it, a Ctrl-click on it clears it, a Shift-click links it in chat.
- **Keys and gamepad**: Set Or Clear The Map Pin At The Middle, and Next Mark, which turns the globe to the marks in the picture one after another. The mark at the middle of the picture shows its tooltip and takes the press.

## 0.2.0

The window and the controls.

- **The window is sized by its corner**, and **Full Screen** fills the screen with it (`/globe full`). Its size and place are remembered. Escape closes it.
- **Go To**: a list of the continents and their zones; picking one turns and fits the globe.
- **Key bindings** for everything the mouse does, under Options → Keybindings → Patchwerk Forever: open or close, Where I Am, Full Screen Or Window, Go To, Fit The Zone At The Middle, Step Out, turning and zooming. With keys, the middle of the picture is the pointer: a cross marks it, and the zone there is outlined and named.
- **A gamepad turns the globe** while it is open and you are not in combat: the left stick turns it, the right stick or the triggers zoom, and the four face buttons fit the zone at the middle (or press the mark there), step out (and close the window from the whole globe), go to the next mark, and set or clear the map pin. Every other button does what it is bound to. **Gamepad Turns The Globe** turns this off. With Console Port, the globe's bindings are also listed among its own.
- Immersive Plainsrunning counts the globe as an open window: the interface comes back while it is open.

## 0.1.0

- The ground, zoomed right in: the minimap's pictures are drawn where there is land and fade out to the parchment sea over the tiles round it, where they used to end in square edges out at sea. The open sea stays parchment.
- Zoomed in, only the ground in the picture is looked at, not every tile of the world: less work each drawing.
- Zones: the one under the pointer is outlined and named; a click turns and zooms the globe to fit it; a right-click steps back out, to the continent and then the whole globe.
- Hand-made art: old paper over the whole world; a compass rose, sea serpents and ships in ink on the open sea, sharp as you zoom in; the brass ring and wooden stand of a desk globe round the ball. Zoomed right out, the whole desk globe is in view; zooming in, the ball fills the window.
- A corner of land drawn out at sea north-east of Kalimdor is gone: a rim of flat dry ground along the edges of three ocean tiles in the game's terrain, now left out of the pictures.
