# The road to 1.0 for the beta addons

**Update, 2026-11-13, later the same day.** The build was played, reported as all working and ready
to go live, and the versions were chosen: Tracker 1.0.0 and Sparkle 1.0.0 are full releases; Routes stays beta
(0.14.0). Built since this list was written: T11 (next and previous quest bindings) and T13, R8, S6
(Reset To Defaults). Every "Confirm" below is taken as confirmed by that report for the whole. What is
left of this list has moved to `ROADMAP.md`.

Written 2026-11-13, asked for as a feature list to take the beta addons to 1.0.
Three addons are in beta: **Tracker (Thread) 0.9.0**, **Routes (Thread) 0.14.0** and
**Sparkle (Thread) 0.12.0**. The base (2.2.0) and the Stitches (2.0.1) are not.

This is a proposal. The Tracker's bar for 1.0 is the user's own, from the interview of 2026-11-13;
the lists for Routes and Sparkle, and every "should" and "later", are mine and open to change.

Each item is one of:

- **Confirm**: built, passes the checks outside the game, not yet seen working in the game.
- **Build**: not built.
- **Decide**: needs an answer before anything is built.

## What 1.0 means, for all three

1. Every feature seen working in the game.
2. A week of ordinary play with no Lua errors and no "action blocked" messages, a dungeon included.
3. It works with nothing else installed: no EllesmereUI, Console Port, RestedXP, and for the
   Tracker and Sparkle no Routes (Thread).
4. Everything it shows is in standard wording (done for settings, menus, chat lines and bindings;
   the READMEs' prose is not).
5. It can be driven from a gamepad as well as a mouse.

## A suggested order

1. **Play what is built** (T1 to T10, R1, R2, S1 to S5, A1) and report. Most of the list is "Confirm",
   so this decides how much real work is left.
2. **Fix what that finds.**
3. **Small builds that every addon shares**: Reset To Defaults (T13, R8, S6), gamepad quest
   selection (T11).
4. **Sparkle to 1.0** (S7, then its week).
5. **Tracker to 1.0** (T12 after the week of play, T14 when the art is there).
6. **Routes to 1.0** last: R3 and R4 are the real work, and R6 needs play on the other continent.
