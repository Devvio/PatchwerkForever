# Patchwerk Forever: Stitch (MelloUI Route)

## 2.0.0

First release as an addon of its own. Until Patchwerk Forever 1.0.1, the additions to MelloUI's Route were part of that addon; its history up to then is in Patchwerk Forever's changelog.

- Needs **Patchwerk Forever 2.0.0** or later (the base), which holds the settings page and the saved data.
- Nothing changed in what it does. Your settings and saved data from 1.x are used as they are.
- The Route actions are still listed in Console Port's bindings when Console Port is installed; this part now finds Console Port by itself.
- An icon in the game's addon list.
