# Patchwerk Forever: Thread (Globe)

## 0.0.1

- A first try: the world on a globe you can spin and zoom, drawn from the game's own minimap pictures, with your place marked and the drawing numbers under it.
- The parchment look: each continent's painted world map on the globe, its edges fading into a parchment sea (Map Style: Parchment, the default; Minimap Pictures as before). Larger continents. Where I Am glides there.
- Drawn in pieces that split as you zoom, so the globe stays round and nothing folds over its edge; the ground fades in over the parchment as you zoom in; zooming toward the pointer, smooth, and by double-click; a drag carries on after you let go; the names of continents and zones; an arrow turned the way you face.
- The world painted by our own tools from the game's terrain, one picture of the whole sphere (in place of the game's continent maps): zone tints, hills, inked coasts with ripple lines, zone borders, paper; the seas named. Pieces across the globe's edge are drawn to it, not cut short.
- Each continent in detail (about 8 yards a pixel), fading in over the world picture as you zoom in; the minimap's pictures come in after it, closer.
