# Patchwerk Forever: Stitch (MelloUI)

## 2.0.1

- Its options page opens with its picture and a line from Patchwerk, on Patchwerk Forever 2.2.0. Nothing changes in what it does.

## 2.0.0

First release as an addon of its own. Until Patchwerk Forever 1.0.1, the MelloUI game menu button fix and the trim to Route and the Quest Tracker were part of that addon; its history up to then is in Patchwerk Forever's changelog.

- Needs **Patchwerk Forever 2.0.0** or later (the base), which holds the settings page and the saved data.
- Nothing changed in what it does. Your settings and saved data from 1.x are used as they are.
- An icon in the game's addon list.
- **Checked against MelloUI 2.1.48.** Since 2.1.46 MelloUI keeps a profile for each character, and which modules are on belongs to the profile. The trim to Route and the Quest Tracker now works once for each profile: a character whose profile was never trimmed is trimmed at its first login, and what you switch back on stays on in that profile. With an older MelloUI it works for the account, as before.
